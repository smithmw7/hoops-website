1. Introduction

Hightop Games LLC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Aces (the "App") on iOS, Android, or web.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited information necessary to provide the App and improve the experience.

Specifically, we may collect or process:

  • Device and local progress: Game state and session data are stored primarily on your device (e.g., via local storage or device preferences). This allows the App to restore your progress when you return.
  • Content delivery: If the App fetches content or updates from our servers or a content-delivery service, we or that service may receive minimal technical data such as app version or request timestamps. We do not use this to identify you personally.
  • Optional account information: If we offer optional sign-in in the future (e.g., Sign in with Apple), we would receive only the identifiers or information provided by that provider. We do not currently require an account to play.
  • Analytics (if any): If we use analytics, we use anonymous or aggregated data such as session length or device type. We do not sell or use this data to identify you.

We do not collect personal location data, contact lists, or camera/microphone data. The App is designed to be playable without creating an account or providing personal information.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to provide and maintain gameplay, improve the App, fix technical issues, and comply with law or protect our rights. We do not sell or rent your information.

4. Third-Party Services

Aces may use third-party services for content delivery or app distribution (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play). Those platforms process data under their own privacy policies. If we add optional sign-in, that provider's privacy policy will apply to the data they share with us.

5. Data Retention and Security

Progress and game state are stored on your device; you can clear them via device settings or by uninstalling the App. We retain only what is necessary to operate the App or comply with law. We take reasonable measures to protect information but cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

6. Children's Privacy

Aces is intended for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child has provided personal data, we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights and Choices

You can clear App data via your device settings or by uninstalling the App. For questions about your data or to request deletion of any data we hold, contact us at privacy@hightopgames.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the App and on our website, and the effective date will be revised accordingly.
