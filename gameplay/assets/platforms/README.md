# Platform navigation marks

The Apple, Google Play, Steam, PlayStation, and Roblox SVGs are cached from
Simple Icons v16 for the private Gameplay research interface. Simple Icons is
distributed under CC0, but individual brand marks may remain subject to
trademark or brand-guideline restrictions. Review those rights before public
distribution.

Source pattern:
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@v16/icons/<slug>.svg`

The Nintendo, Xbox, UGC, browser, arcade, tabletop, and All Games marks are
small original interface glyphs rendered in `src/App.tsx`; they are not claimed
as official brand logos.
